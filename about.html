Who We Are

Built on Quality & Trust

Lifewear Steel Products is a wholesale and retail supplier of stainless steel and aluminium products based in San Juan, Trinidad. We've built a reputation for quality products, reliable service, and competitive pricing across Trinidad and Tobago.

We supply contractors, fabricators, manufacturers, and individuals with the right grade and gauge for every application — from food-grade stainless for commercial kitchens to marine-grade aluminium for boat railings.

Our mission: provide the best material at a fair price, with the expert advice and delivery service to make your project a success.
